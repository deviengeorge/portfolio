Hessah System was a SaaS experiment: a management tool for private tutors in Egypt, from attendance and payments to exam performance and parent reports. I built it to test whether the idea was worth turning into a product. It was **never launched**.

**At a glance**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Role** | Full-stack developer and product owner |
| **Status** | Experiment, never launched |
| **Goal** | Test a SaaS idea end to end: product, build, and business |
| **Stack** | Laravel, FilamentPHP, Livewire, MySQL, Redis |

## The Idea

Private tutors in Egypt often manage dozens of students across several classes with notebooks and WhatsApp groups. The hypothesis was that a simple, branded dashboard could replace that: fewer missed payments, accurate attendance, and a view of each student's progress.

## What I Built

- **Attendance tracking** — per-class, per-session records with absence notifications to parents
- **Payment management** — fee tracking, payment history, and overdue alerts
- **Exam performance** — score entry with trends and comparisons
- **Parent communication** — automated progress reports
- **Multi-class support** — different subjects, schedules, and student groups
- **Per-tutor dashboard** — branding, icons, and color themes, with multi-role access

## Technical Architecture

- **Backend:** Laravel with FilamentPHP for fast admin development
- **Frontend:** Livewire for a reactive UI without a separate JavaScript app
- **Data:** MySQL, with Redis for sessions and frequently used data
- **Notifications:** Laravel queues and events for parent notifications

## What I Learned

- How to scope a SaaS from idea to working product on my own
- Business strategy, marketing, and product management for a software product
- That building the product is the smaller half of a SaaS: finding and keeping users is the larger half
